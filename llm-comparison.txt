My models.py is the simpler of the two by far. It has five tables that match the
assignment list exactly (InstructorProfile, StudentProfile, Course,
Enrollment, Lecture, QRCodeUpload), each with only the fields the spec asks
for, and no extra logic other tha  a __str__ method and a tiny helper that
generates a random QR string. The LLM version (GPT 6 Sol High) is about four
times longer: it uses a single UserProfile table with a role column instead of
two profile tables, adds a separate QRCode table with hashed tokens that expire
in a minute, and puts a lot of application logic into the models like
clean() and save() validation, a review workflow with pending/verified/rejected
status, photo hashing to catch duplicate uploads, and attendance-rate
properties. That makes it the more complete model, especially on the
anti-cheating requirement, but most of it goes beyond what HW3 asks for. It also misses one part of the
spec: the lecture link on an upload is required, not optional. Mine was easier
to implement. makemigrations and migrate worked on the first try, while the
LLM's would fail in my environment because ImageField needs the Pillow
library, and its save() overrides would need testing before they could be
trusted. Overall the LLM output is a good source of ideas for later homeworks and a more thorough model,
but my model is the one that fits this assignment.
